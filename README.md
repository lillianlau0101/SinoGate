## 🌏 What is SinoGate?

SinoGate is a cross-border private markets distribution platform that connects **international investors and RIAs** to **China's best quantitative and alternative fund strategies** — fully packaged, compliant, and ready to allocate.

The problem is simple: world-class Chinese quant managers generating 15–25% net annualized returns are **structurally invisible** to global capital. Wrong fund formats. Wrong language. Wrong compliance framework.

SinoGate fixes the plumbing.

---

## 🚨 The Problem We Solve

| Pain Point | Reality |
|---|---|
| 🔒 **Access** | Foreign investors have no easy path to top Chinese quant managers |
| 🗂️ **Packaging** | Chinese PFM/onshore LP structures are incompatible with global platforms |
| 📉 **Diversification** | A-share alpha has near-zero global correlation — yet most portfolios have zero exposure |
| 💸 **Distribution** | Chinese managers have no scalable IR or LP outreach infrastructure for overseas capital |

---

## ✅ What We Do
```
Chinese Manager  →  SinoGate  →  Global Investor
```

**Four core functions:**

1. **Strategy Sourcing** — Identify & vet Chinese quant/alt managers with audited track records  
2. **Compliant Packaging** — Structure into Cayman SPV, UCITS, or interval fund formats  
3. **Distribution** — Connect to global RIAs, family offices & wealth platforms  
4. **IR & Reporting** — English NAV reports, audit coordination, ongoing LP comms

## 💡 The Bigger Thesis

> *"This is an ETF-like shift — whoever owns distribution, wins."*

Private market managers are increasingly constrained in traditional fundraising channels (pension, endowment, SWF). The next growth engine is **wealth platforms, advisors, and retirement accounts**.

SinoGate sits at the intersection of:
- 🇨🇳 China's $3.8T private fund market
- 🌍 Global alternatives demand for uncorrelated return streams
- 📦 The packaging & distribution revolution in private markets
